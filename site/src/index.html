A 2D top-down Counter-Strike clone you play in the browser.

Five-on-five rounds of plant and defuse, seen from above. Buy guns, throw smokes, play against bots or send a friend a four-letter room code.

Play now View source

The game server runs on a free tier that sleeps when nobody is playing. If the page takes a while to open, give it up to a minute to wake up.

What's in it

  • MR12 matches

    First to 13 rounds wins. Teams swap sides at the half, and a 12–12 tie goes to overtime.

  • Round economy

    Win and loss bonuses, kill rewards and a buy menu with rifles, SMGs, pistols, armor and defuse kits.

  • Utility

    HE grenades, flashbangs, smokes that block sight lines, and molotovs that burn an area.

  • Bots

    Quick Match fills both teams with bots at easy, normal or hard. They buy, push sites, plant and defuse.

  • Online rooms

    Create a room, share its four-letter code, and play together. Bots can backfill anyone who leaves.

  • Fog of war

    You only see what your player can see. The server never sends enemies hidden behind walls or smoke.

How to play

  1. Open the game and type a name.
  2. Pick Quick Match vs Bots to jump straight into Dust II.
  3. Or pick Create Room, choose Dust II or Mirage, and send the four-letter code to friends so they can Join.
  4. Press B during freeze time to buy, then go plant or defuse the bomb.

Works best on a desktop browser with a keyboard and mouse.
